## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creator's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description

Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

- Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Provides logistics support.
- Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

- Better prices for farmers.
- Lower prices for consumers.
- Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

We propose to develop a user-friendly digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

The platform will allow farmers to:

1. Register and create their profiles.
2. Upload agricultural products with prices and quantities.
3. Receive orders directly from customers.
4. Manage their products and orders.

Consumers can:

1. Browse available agricultural products.
2. Compare product prices.
3. Place orders directly from farmers.
4. Track their order status.

Innovation and Uniqueness:

- Direct connection between farmers and consumers.
- Reduced dependency on middlemen.
- Transparent product pricing.
- Simple and user-friendly interface.

## Technical Approach

Technologies to be used:

1. Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
2. Backend: Python.
3. Framework: Flask.
4. Database: SQLite.
5. Development Tool: Visual Studio Code.

Methodology and Process:

1. Farmer registration and login.
2. Farmers upload agricultural products.
3. Consumers browse available products.
4. Consumers place orders.
5. Farmers receive and manage orders.
6. Delivery status is updated.

## Feasibility and Viability

Analysis of Feasibility:

- The platform can be developed using affordable technologies.
- Python, HTML, CSS and SQLite are suitable for developing a basic prototype.
- The website can be accessed through computers and mobile devices.

Potential Challenges and Risks:

1. Limited technical knowledge among farmers.
2. Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.
3. Delivery and transportation difficulties.
4. Maintaining accurate product information.

Strategies for Overcoming Challenges:

1. Provide a simple user-friendly interface.
2. Develop a mobile-friendly website.
3. Display clear product details and prices.
4. Provide delivery support.

## Impact and Benefits

Potential Impact on Target Audience:

1. Helps farmers connect directly with customers.
2. Reduces dependency on intermediaries.
3. Improves market accessibility for farmers.
4. Makes agricultural products easily accessible to consumers.

Benefits of the Solution:

Social Benefits:
- Supports farmers and rural communities.

Economic Benefits:
- Better earnings for farmers.
- Affordable prices for consumers.

Environmental Benefits:
- Better transportation planning.
